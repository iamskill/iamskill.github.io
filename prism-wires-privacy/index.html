Prism Wires Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Prism Wires: Circuit Puzzle is an offline circuit puzzle game published by Decor Designs.

Game data

Prism Wires works offline and has no accounts or in-app purchases. The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or Android Advertising ID permissions. The Google Play version uses an internet connection for advertising and privacy choices.

Google AdMob and privacy choices

The Google Play version can show a Google AdMob banner in the level picker. Its ad requests use child-directed treatment, and ads are not personalized. Google’s advertising and privacy tools may process IP addresses (which may indicate an approximate location), ad and app interactions, diagnostics, device or app identifiers, and consent information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and privacy choices. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. When Google requires a privacy choices screen, it is available under Privacy in the game.

Game progress

The app saves completed levels, stars, and daily puzzle results locally on your device. The game does not send this information to Decor Designs or anyone else. To delete this information, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Children’s privacy

The game does not ask children to enter personal information. The same child-directed ad treatment is used for all players. Parents and guardians can contact us with privacy questions.

Google Play and support

Google Play handles downloads and updates under Google’s Privacy Policy. If you email us, we use your email address and message to respond to your request. You can use the same address to request deletion of your support correspondence.
